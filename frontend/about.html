How it Works

Our system bridges the gap between your current skills and your dream career using state-of-the-art Natural Language Processing.

🧠

SBERT Analysis

We use Sentence-BERT (SBERT) to convert your background description into high-dimensional vectors. This allows the AI to understand the context of your experience, not just keywords.

📊

ESCO Integration

Your profile is matched against the European Skills, Competencies, and Occupations (ESCO) framework, ensuring our career suggestions follow global industry standards.

🗺️

Gap Mapping

Once a match is found, the system identifies the specific skills you lack and generates a Personalized Learning Path to help you bridge that gap.
